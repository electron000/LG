Statutory Interpretation

1. Categorical Restraint Proscription

Under Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act, any agreement restraining exercise of lawful trade, business, or profession is void pro tanto. Indian jurisprudence adheres strictly to statutory text, declining to import the English common law "doctrine of reasonableness" to post-termination restrictive covenants.

gavel Key Judicial Benchmark

"The rule is absolute: an employer cannot protect itself against ordinary competition from a former employee through covenants taking effect post severance."

Operational Distinctions

2. In-Term vs. Post-Termination Covenants

Negative stipulations operative during employment tenure do not attract the prohibition of Section 27 unless wholly unconscionable. However, clauses extending beyond resignation or discharge—irrespective of geography, remuneration, or tenure—remain unenforceable in Indian jurisdiction.
